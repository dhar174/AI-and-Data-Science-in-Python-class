Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Explain temporal leakage. | State why a naive sequence baseline matters. | Describe the purpose of recurrent gating.
  2. Worked example: Calculate attention weights for three tokens and show how a causal mask changes the available context.
  3. Guided practice: Query/key/value analogy; small attention calculation; mask demonstration; transformer block map; LLM bridge.
  4. Assessment: Attention mechanism explanation and architecture comparison
  5. Homework: Annotate the transformer diagram and write two recurrence/attention tradeoffs. (45 minutes)
