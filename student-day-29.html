Tonight's goal

Compare routing, parallel, supervisor, hierarchical, plan–execute, reflection, Reflexion, and bounded tree-of-thought patterns using observable traces and explicit authority boundaries.

By the end, keep:
  • Two pattern traces identify input, authority, state, evidence, next destination, budget, and stop reason.
  • The comparison distinguishes deterministic control-flow evidence from live model reasoning quality.
  • The architecture decision names a simpler baseline, a reason for coordination, and a fail-closed condition.
