Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Explain why vector proximity is not the same as relevance. | Name one metadata-filtering decision. | Describe one negation failure from yesterday’s investigation.
  2. Worked example: Answer questions from a small policy corpus while comparing chunk sizes and inspecting retrieved passages before generation.
  3. Guided practice: Chunking experiment; retrieval inspection; grounded-answer lab; unsupported-answer diagnosis.
  4. Assessment: RAG evidence audit
  5. Homework: Complete the RAG fault log and add three grounded evaluation cases. (60 minutes)
