Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Match classification task to output and loss. | Explain why a baseline remains necessary. | State one first-batch validation check.
  2. Worked example: Diagnose three curve pairs: underfitting, overfitting, and unstable optimization.
  3. Guided practice: Learning-rate experiment; overfitting-curve interpretation; regularization intervention; experiment-log critique.
  4. Assessment: Training-diagnostics notebook
  5. Homework: Run or interpret one controlled optimization experiment and update the model card draft. (55 minutes)
