Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Define leakage. | Assign train, validation, and test roles. | Identify the misleading visual feature in yesterday’s repair.
  2. Worked example: Predict house prices with numeric and categorical features using a pipeline and compare MAE with RMSE.
  3. Guided practice: Pipeline think-aloud; scaling and encoding comparison; residual investigation; error-metric decision exercise.
  4. Assessment: Regression pipeline lab
  5. Homework: Complete the residual analysis and explain one feature-engineering risk. (55 minutes)
