Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Explain one attention limitation. | Identify the capstone’s most important failure mode. | State the difference between training and inference.
  2. Worked example: Turn a capstone prediction workflow into a minimal repeatable inference contract: input, preprocessing, model, output, and caveats.
  3. Guided practice: Capstone demonstration; inference rerun; model-card conference; token/context bridge retrieval.
  4. Assessment: Module 2 capstone rubric, model card, individual defense, and bridge assessment
  5. Homework: Submit reflection and complete the LLM readiness diagnostic. (40 minutes)
