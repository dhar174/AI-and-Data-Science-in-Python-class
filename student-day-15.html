Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Verify one batch before training. | Explain what a gradient represents. | Distinguish model parameters from hyperparameters.
  2. Worked example: Compare a one-output regression network with a three-class classifier and repair mismatched losses and labels.
  3. Guided practice: Output/loss matching sort; training-loop think-aloud; regression/classification comparison; decision-boundary analysis.
  4. Assessment: Neural task/loss comparison lab
  5. Homework: Finish the comparison lab and state the capstone output/loss design. (55 minutes)
