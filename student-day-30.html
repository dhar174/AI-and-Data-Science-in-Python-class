Tonight's goal

Use a shared specification/review workflow with coding assistants and carry a bounded change through a truthful local browser-to-model deployment.

By the end, keep:
  • The assistant change is bounded by a written specification and its diff preserves existing session behavior.
  • Focused checks and command output are inspected, with a clear distinction between attempted and observed test results.
  • The deployment transcript includes model identity, health caveat, browser/API path, session checks, failure case, and recovery route.
