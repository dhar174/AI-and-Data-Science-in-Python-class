Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Explain why preprocessing belongs inside a pipeline. | Contrast MAE and RMSE. | Identify the role of a baseline.
  2. Worked example: Tune a classifier for a medical-screening scenario while comparing recall, precision, calibration, and resource costs.
  3. Guided practice: Confusion-matrix misconception sort; threshold experiment; cross-validation; bounded grid/random search; final test protocol.
  4. Assessment: Classification and tuning decision memo
  5. Homework: Finish the tuning memo and add one contamination warning to the error log. (55 minutes)
