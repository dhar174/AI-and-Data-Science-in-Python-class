Tonight's goal

Demonstrate a bounded generative-AI application and defend its evidence, safeguards, deployment route, limitations, human oversight, and—when selected—the documented PEFT/QLoRA/SFT/DPO comparison.

Success criteria:
  • Students demonstrate the application, reproduce one representative and one failure test, explain one failure and control, state where the system should not be used, and defend the selected route against a simpler baseline.

Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Name one tested safeguard. | State the capstone’s most important residual risk. | Explain one human-approval boundary and one route-specific comparison.
  2. Worked example: Evaluate a final application claim by tracing it through input, model/retrieval/tool behavior, base or adapted state, test evidence, safeguard, fallback, and residual risk.
  3. Guided practice: Clean-start demonstration; route and fallback review; base/SFT/DPO evidence review when applicable; individual defense; peer transfer questions; course synthesis.
  4. Assessment: Module 3 capstone rubric, individual defense, reproducibility check, and final reflection
  5. Homework: Submit the final artifact, required route documentation, final evaluation table, and a 30-day learning continuation plan. (45 minutes)
