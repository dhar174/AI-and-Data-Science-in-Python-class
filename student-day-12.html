Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: State the train/validation/test roles. | Explain one consequence of leakage. | Describe a model parameter from Module 1.
  2. Worked example: Calculate a two-input, two-neuron forward pass and compare linear versus ReLU activation.
  3. Guided practice: Cross-module retrieval; neuron computation; activation comparison; forward-pass walkthrough; misconception check.
  4. Assessment: Neural-network concept map and forward-pass trace
  5. Homework: Complete two forward-pass examples and annotate the concept map. (45 minutes)
