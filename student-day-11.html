Tonight's goal

Defend a classical data-science solution and transfer its concepts to tensors, gradients, and neural-network learning.

Success criteria:
  • Students demonstrate the artifact, defend one design decision, identify one limitation, and explain one concept that transfers forward.

Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Define leakage in one sentence. | Name the project baseline and why it is credible. | Identify one result that should not be generalized.
  2. Worked example: Translate a linear model pipeline into the vocabulary of tensors, parameters, loss, gradients, and optimization.
  3. Guided practice: Showcase; evidence-focused peer questions; rubric conference; tensor/gradient bridge retrieval.
  4. Assessment: Module 1 capstone rubric, individual defense, and bridge assessment
  5. Homework: Write a capstone reflection and complete the neural-network readiness diagnostic. (40 minutes)
