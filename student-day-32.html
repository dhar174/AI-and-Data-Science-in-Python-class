Day 32 · Responsible AI, safety, code review, and capstone revision

5:30 p.m.–10:00 p.m. · 240 instructional minutes · 30-minute break

Back to hub

Tonight's goal

Audit an AI application for correctness, privacy, security, bias, provenance, human oversight, and appropriate use; for adapted models, test overfitting, data quality, memorization, catastrophic behavior changes, and alignment risks before revising the capstone.

Success criteria:
  • Students document a concrete risk, test it with safe synthetic or held-out data, implement or recommend a control, retest, and explain residual risk and human oversight.

Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: State two capstone evaluation criteria. | Explain why source computation governs CSV claims. | Identify one adaptation risk or conversational-state risk.
  2. Worked example: Red-team a knowledge assistant or adapted small model for prompt injection, private-data disclosure, unsupported advice, misleading citations, overfitting, and behavior drift.
  3. Guided practice: AI code-review critique; threat and misuse scenarios; provenance check; prompt-injection and private-data tests; adaptation overfitting/data-quality/alignment-risk audit; revision sprint; creative-use survey.
  4. Assessment: Safety/red-team report, code-review memo, adaptation-risk audit, and capstone build review
  5. Homework: Complete priority revisions, model/system card, adaptation-risk audit when applicable, and individual technical-defense notes. (80 minutes)
