Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: State two postconditions from the wrangling notebook. | Explain why a unique ID should not be averaged. | Distinguish filtering rows from selecting columns.
  2. Worked example: Investigate whether late deliveries differ by region using a join, grouped summary, distribution plot, and uncertainty statement.
  3. Guided practice: SQL query ladder; database-to-pandas bridge; distribution comparison; correlation and anomaly investigation.
  4. Assessment: EDA evidence memo with reproducible SQL
  5. Homework: Revise the memo and complete an interleaved SQL/statistics retrieval set. (50 minutes)
