Tonight's goal

Trace text through a transformer, compare model-size tradeoffs, and leave with versioned evidence for a Hugging Face route.

By the end, keep:
  • The transformer trace labels tokenization, embeddings, attention weights, masking, block updates, and decoding.
  • The decision brief names at least two tradeoffs and explains why a smaller model can be the better engineering choice.
  • The evidence record includes model, tokenizer, revision or version, device route, and a limitation of the observed output.
