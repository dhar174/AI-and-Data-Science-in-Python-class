Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Distinguish training from inference. | Name the parts of a repeatable inference contract. | Explain why validation remains necessary after deep learning.
  2. Worked example: Compare tokenization and next-token distributions for two short prompts with ambiguous wording.
  3. Guided practice: Generative-AI landscape; tokenization demonstration; context experiment; claim audit.
  4. Assessment: LLM explanation and misconception audit
  5. Homework: Write a 150-word LLM explanation for a nontechnical stakeholder and flag two limitations. (40 minutes)
