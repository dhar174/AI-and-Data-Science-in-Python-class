Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Write one query that would support the EDA memo. | Explain correlation without claiming causation. | Name the grain of yesterday’s example table.
  2. Worked example: Convert a customer-retention question into features, label, temporal split, baseline, and two honest charts.
  3. Guided practice: Chart repair studio; feature/label sort; train/validation/test simulation; leakage case discussion.
  4. Assessment: Visualization critique and ML concept check
  5. Homework: Repair one chart and write a leakage example from the chosen capstone domain. (40 minutes)
