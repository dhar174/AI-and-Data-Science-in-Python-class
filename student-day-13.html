Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Trace yesterday’s forward pass. | Explain why nonlinearity matters. | Recall the purpose of validation data.
  2. Worked example: Implement the same two-layer classifier first with NumPy operations and then with `torch.nn`.
  3. Guided practice: NumPy forward pass; tensor/module tour; paired implementation; parameter inspection.
  4. Assessment: NumPy/PyTorch comparison notebook
  5. Homework: Complete the translation notebook and explain one abstraction PyTorch provides. (50 minutes)
