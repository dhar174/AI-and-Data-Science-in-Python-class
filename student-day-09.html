Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Explain one clustering limitation. | Contrast supervised and unsupervised evidence. | Recall the three protected roles of the test set.
  2. Worked example: Compare a random forest, popularity recommender, collaborative filter, and TF-IDF classifier across four business scenarios.
  3. Guided practice: Bagging/boosting comparison; recommender cold-start scenario; TF-IDF classification lab; bias and feedback-loop review.
  4. Assessment: Application-pattern design review
  5. Homework: Complete the design review and prepare the capstone reproducibility checklist. (45 minutes)
