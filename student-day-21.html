Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Explain causal masking. | Contrast recurrence and attention. | Name one limitation of attention visualization.
  2. Worked example: Compare grid-world reward learning, node classification, adversarial image generation, and denoising generation using a common decision matrix.
  3. Guided practice: RL episode trace; graph message-passing simulation; GAN/diffusion contrast; capstone reproducibility and failure audit.
  4. Assessment: Architecture comparison brief and capstone build review
  5. Homework: Finish capstone priority revisions and rehearse the individual technical defense. (75 minutes)
