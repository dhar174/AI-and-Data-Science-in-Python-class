Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Diagnose an overfitting curve. | Explain a controlled experiment. | Name one reason validation evidence may change across runs.
  2. Worked example: Adapt a pretrained image model to a small two-class dataset and compare frozen-feature and fine-tuned runs.
  3. Guided practice: Filter/receptive-field visualization; pretrained-model workflow; freezing/fine-tuning comparison; image error gallery.
  4. Assessment: CNN and transfer-learning investigation
  5. Homework: Complete the error gallery and document the transfer-learning decision. (60 minutes)
