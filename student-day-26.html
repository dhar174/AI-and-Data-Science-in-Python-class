Day 26 · Prompt engineering, DPO, and base–SFT–DPO evaluation

5:30 p.m.–10:00 p.m. · 240 instructional minutes · 30-minute break

Back to hub

Tonight's goal

Specify and test prompts, format preference pairs, and run or inspect DPO after SFT so that base, SFT, and DPO behavior are compared with fixed qualitative and quantitative evidence.

Success criteria:
  • The comparison uses a fixed held-out set, reports base/SFT/DPO outputs, includes at least one appropriate quantitative metric, and explains what the train[:64]/train[:32] reduced experiment cannot establish.

Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Trace the Day 25 LoRA/quantization configuration. | Name the parts of a prompt contract and an instruction example. | Explain why one attractive generation is not an evaluation.
  2. Worked example: Use the existing Tiny LLM SFT and DPO Demo to inspect or run SFT on train[:64], apply a short DPO run on train[:32] chosen/rejected pairs, and compare the same prompts across the base, SFT, and DPO states.
  3. Guided practice: Prompt decomposition; chosen/rejected preference-pair inspection; DPO intuition and objective; DPO after SFT; relationship among SFT, DPO, RLHF, and PPO-style alignment; qualitative comparison; task/preference metrics; limitations and alignment-risk discussion.
  4. Assessment: Prompt test suite and base–SFT–DPO comparison report
  5. Homework: Complete the base–SFT–DPO comparison table, report one qualitative change and one quantitative result, and state two limitations or alignment risks. (60 minutes)
