Session outline

What to do today

  1. Retrieval and goals: Define a false positive in yesterday’s scenario. | Explain why tuning must occur inside cross-validation. | State when the test set is used.
  2. Worked example: Cluster customers before and after scaling, project with PCA, and compare whether the same narrative survives.
  3. Guided practice: K-means simulation; scaling sensitivity; PCA projection; stability check; cluster-story critique.
  4. Assessment: Unsupervised interpretation brief
  5. Homework: Revise the interpretation brief and retrieve classification-versus-clustering distinctions. (40 minutes)
