Tonight's goal

Choose and document a model adaptation route while distinguishing memory-saving inference, LoRA/QLoRA, and genuine quantization-aware training.

By the end, keep:
  • The configuration record names the base model, trainable parameters, target modules, quantization route, steps, and evaluation case.
  • The learner can explain why adapter reload logits matching is a lifecycle check, not evidence of quality improvement.
  • The route decision states whether prompting/RAG, LoRA, QLoRA, or QAT is justified and identifies the compute and evaluation limits.
